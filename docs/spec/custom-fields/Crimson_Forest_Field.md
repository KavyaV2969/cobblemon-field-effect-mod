# Crimson Forest Field

> **Entry:** “The red flora preying...”

## Mapping

| Minecraft biome | Field |
|---|---|
| `minecraft:crimson_forest` | Crimson Forest Field |

## Fusion Basis

**Forest + Volcanic + Corrosive Mist + light Colosseum**.

## General Effects

- Fire moves: **1.3×** — “The Nether's heat strengthened the attack!”
- Grass moves: **1.3×** — “The crimson growth strengthened the attack!”
- Poison moves: **1.3×** — “The crimson spores strengthened the attack!”
- Special Bug moves: **1.2×** — “The swarm emerged from the crimson growth!”
- Ice moves: **0.5×** — “The Nether's heat softened the attack...”
- Water moves: **0.8×** — “The Nether's heat weakened the attack...”
- Pokémon cannot be frozen; Snow ends when established.
- Special Flying moves gain **Poison sub-typing** for type effectiveness — “The crimson spores clung to the attack!”

## Piglin Bloodlust

Whenever a Pokémon directly KOs an opposing Pokémon:

- The attacker gains **Attack +1**.
- This is a field effect, not the Moxie ability; it may stack with Moxie.
- “The Piglins roared for blood!”

## Good as Gold

When a Pokémon with **Good as Gold** enters the field:

- Speed **+1**.
- Special Attack **+1**.
- “The Piglins erupted at the sight of gold!”

Additional gold interactions:

- Pay Day: **1.5×** — “The Piglins scrambled for the coins!”
- Make It Rain: **1.3×** — “The Piglins went wild for the shower of gold!”

## Crimson Vines — Priority Punishment

All damaging moves with **positive priority** have their final accuracy multiplied by **0.8**.

If the move misses because of this field penalty:

1. “...But the red vines grabbed its limbs!”
2. “[Pokémon] lost control and crashed!”
3. Apply the battle engine's standard **High Jump Kick-style crash damage**.

Moves that inherently bypass accuracy checks are not affected.

## Inherited Ability Effects

Selected parent-field effects:

- Grass Pelt, Leaf Guard, Overgrow and Swarm are activated.
- Effect Spore activation chance becomes **60%**.
- Merciless, Poison Heal and Toxic Boost are activated, following Corrosive Mist behavior.
- Grounded Flash Fire and Well-Baked Body users receive their Volcanic-style activation at end of turn.
- Magma Armor raises Defense by 1 stage on switch-in.
- Steam Engine may gain its Volcanic end-of-turn Speed activation where supported.

## Selected Move Effects

- Acid Spray / Clear Smog / Smog: **1.5×** — “The crimson fumes strengthened the attack!”
- Barb Barrage / Venoshock: double in power regardless of target status.
- Toxic: **100% accuracy**.
- Fire Spin: **1/6 max HP** residual damage.
- Will-O-Wisp: **100% accuracy**.
- Forest-style cutting/slashing moves that gain Grass sub-typing retain those interactions.
- Power Whip / Vine Whip : **1.5x** and gain fire sub-typing - “Blazing vines joined the attack!”

## Standard Field Hooks

- Mimicry / Camouflage / Terrain Pulse: **Fire**.
- Nature Power: **Power Whip**.
- Secret Power: may inflict Burn.
- Shelter: halves Fire damage.
- Elemental Seed: **Attack +1, Special Attack +1, Speed +1**, then applies Taunt to the user.

## Reference Basis

Selective fusion of **Forest Field**, **Volcanic Field**, **Corrosive Mist Field** and **Colosseum Field**. The KO reward is customized to a fixed Attack boost to represent Piglin spectators rather than copying Colosseum's highest-stat reward.
