# Warped Forest Field

> **Entry:** “The forest twitches grotesquely...”

## Mapping

| Minecraft biome | Field |
|---|---|
| `minecraft:warped_forest` | Warped Forest Field |

## Fusion Basis

**Forest + Dimensional + Wasteland + light Volcanic**.

## General Effects

- Grass moves: **1.5×** — “The forestry strengthened the attack!”
- Special Bug moves: **1.5×** — “The attack spread throughout the forest!”
- Dark moves: **1.5×** — “The darkness is here...”
- Ghost moves: **1.2×** — “The evil aura powered up the attack...”
- Fairy moves: **0.5×** — “The evil aura depleted the attack!”
- Fire moves: **1.3×** — “The Nether's heat strengthened the attack!”
- Ice moves: **0.5×** — “The Nether's heat softened the attack...”
- Water moves: **0.8×** — “The Nether's heat weakened the attack...”
- Pokémon cannot be frozen; Snow ends when established.
- Damaging Grass moves additionally gain **Dark sub-typing** for type effectiveness, using Rejuvenation's normal secondary-type field rules — “The warped flora twisted the attack!”
- Weather effects fail while the field is active.

## Ability Effects

Inherited where supported:

- Grass Pelt, Leaf Guard, Overgrow and Swarm are activated.
- Effect Spore activation chance becomes **60%**.
- Sap Sipper restores **1/16 max HP** each turn.
- Rattled additionally raises Speed by 1 stage on switch-in.
- Unnerve additionally lowers opposing Speed by 1 stage on switch-in.
- Poison Heal and Toxic Boost are activated for grounded users.

## Move Effects

### Warped Flora

- Power Whip / Vine Whip: **1.5×** — “The warped roots lashed out!”
- Leech Seed drains **1/4 max HP** each turn — “The warped roots burrowed deeper!”
- Forest-style cutting/slashing moves that gain Grass sub-typing retain that interaction; the resulting Grass component also carries the field's Dark sub-typing behavior.

### Dimensional Distortion

- Hyperspace Fury, Hyperspace Hole, Shadow Force and Spacial Rend: **1.5×** — “Space warped between the twisting trees!”
- Dark Pulse / Night Daze: **1.2×** and cannot miss — “The warped forest folded around the attack!”
- Gravity, Magic Room, Trick Room and Wonder Room use Dimensional-style unstable durations where supported.

## Standard Field Hooks

- Mimicry / Camouflage / Terrain Pulse: **Dark**.
- Nature Power: **Wood Hammer**.
- Secret Power: may flinch.
- Shelter: halves Dark damage.
- Magical Seed: **Defense +1, Special Defense +1, Speed -1 and applies Ingrain to the user**

Selective fusion of **Forest Field**, **Dimensional Field**, **Wasteland** and **Volcanic Field**, modeled after Rejuvenation's existing hybrid-field approach rather than stacking every parent-field rule.
